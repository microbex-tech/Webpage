About Us

Microbex is a Food & Technology company driven by a simple belief: better food should be accessible to everyone.

We believe that making better food accessible takes more than developing good products. It requires understanding what people need, sourcing the right ingredients, building efficient supply chains, and ensuring the right products reach the right people.

Through our Food & Nutrition division, we develop food products guided by nutritional value, taste, and convenience. We draw inspiration from traditional grains and ingredients to create products suited to modern lifestyles.

Through our Technology Solutions division, we build and apply technology to help food and agriculture businesses improve efficiency, reduce waste, understand customer needs, and make better decisions.

At Microbex, food and technology are two parts of the same vision: transforming the food ecosystem to make better food more accessible, one meaningful step at a time.
